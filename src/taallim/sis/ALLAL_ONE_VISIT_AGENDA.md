# Allal — One-Visit Agenda (SIS demo + Pilot ask)

*Goal: leave with **two yeses** — (1) a one-trimester **SIS pilot** on 1–2 classes, and (2) **Allal as pilot site #1** for the 6-week Ta'allim English study. Target length ~45 minutes.*

---

## Before you go — what to bring
- [ ] Laptop charged; **mockup open full-screen on the Dashboard** (your most polished asset), with the Access `.accdb`/`.accde` open in the background as the "you own this" proof.
- [ ] **Two students pre-picked** to contrast the dual track — one **academic** (e.g. 1AS) and one **vocational** (e.g. BTS Informatique).
- [ ] **Bulletin tested once** beforehand — or demo the bulletin **on the mockup** if the live schema remap isn't confirmed yet.
- [ ] **Printed:** the one-page value sheet (leave-behind) + the **consent-form drafts (Ar/Fr)** for the study.
- [ ] **USB backup** of the build + a **sample bulletin PDF** (day-of fallback).
- [ ] Your **pricing number decided in advance** (see below).

---

## The visit — timed
| Time | Segment | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **0–5** | **Reconnect & frame** | Warm hello — "I built this out of my time here. Two quick things: a system I'd love to show you, and a small favour for a study." |
| **5–20** | **SIS demo (~8-min click-through)** | Dashboard → **Arabic toggle** (wow #1) → a student record → **dual-track bulletin** (academic vs vocational; Ministry line adapts) → **one-click PDF for a whole class** (wow #2). Run it on the mockup; gesture to the Access build at the close. |
| **20–30** | **The two asks** | **Ask 1 — SIS pilot:** one trimester, **1–2 classes, free**, shaped by their feedback. **Ask 2 — research site #1:** host the **6-week Ta'allim study** (1AM–4AM), ~2 classes + a control, **minimal teacher load**. Keep them distinct — one is an admin tool, one is student learning. |
| **30–38** | **Money & logistics** | Pricing posture (below) + what you need from them: **classes, a teacher contact, the consent route, start dates**. |
| **38–45** | **Close** | Agree a **pilot start date** + named classes + a teacher contact; leave the value sheet; thank them. |

---

## The pricing ask — posture + numbers
- **Lead with the free pilot, not a price:** *"The one-trimester pilot is free — you only license it if your staff say they need it."* Removes risk, earns the testimonial.
- **Signal the model** so there's no sticker shock later: a **one-time license — owned, offline, no subscription** — plus an **optional annual support/updates** fee (Ministry-report changes, tweaks).
- **Anchor on value, not features:** *"Every trimestre your staff spend [X] hours computing averages and writing bulletins by hand — this turns that into seconds."* Let the saved hours justify the price.
- **Have your number ready** (don't quote first; only if asked): a **one-time school license** at **[set your figure — tie it to a fraction of one trimester's admin-time savings; localise in DZD]**, with optional yearly support ≈ **15–20%** of that. *Decide the exact figure before you walk in.*
- **If pressed:** *"Let's let the pilot define the scope, then I'll price it fairly for Allal."*

---

## Have ready — likely questions
- **Cost?** → "One-time, affordable, you own it — no subscription. The pilot's free; we price after."
- **Our data?** → "Stays on your machines, offline. You control the file."
- **Teacher training?** → "Forms are simple and bilingual; I'll do a short handover — and I'm local."
- **Does it really do both tracks?** → show both bulletins live.
- **Research burden?** → "~2 classes, 6 weeks; the app does the teaching — teachers run it and answer a short questionnaire."
