## Lens A — Admin time saved (the value you create)

**Staff cost of time:** a secondary teacher earns ≈ **88,000–106,000 DZD/month** (2025 official scales; broader range 60k–120k). At ~96,000 DZD/month ÷ ~160 h ≈ **~600 DZD/hour** loaded cost. *(Source: dnalgerie / algerie-eco, Jan 2025; Décret exécutif 25-54.)*

**Time recovered** (mostly bulletins: averages, ranks, mentions, transcription — three times a year — plus attendance/records):

> **Formula:** students × minutes saved/student/trimester × 3 ÷ 60 × 600 DZD, + ~30% admin overhead.

**Worked example (≈250-student school, ~10 min/student/trimester):**
- 250 × 10 × 3 = 7,500 min = 125 h → + 30% overhead ≈ **160 h/year**
- 160 h × 600 DZD ≈ **~96,000 DZD/year** in recovered staff time.

**Payback:** a 45,000 DZD one-time license pays for itself in **< 6 months**, then saves ~96,000 DZD **every year after, free**. (Scale up for a larger school; the per-student method self-adjusts.) This is the number that wins the room — frame the price against hours-on-the-calculator, not features.

## Lens B — What a private school can bear

- **Local bilingual private tuition ≈ 100,000–500,000 DZD/student/year** (Allal is local, not international → mid-band ~150k–250k). *(Source: zoomalgerie / ABNews / vitaminedz, 2025–26.)*
- A ~250-student school at ~200,000 DZD ≈ **~50M DZD/year revenue**.
- A **45,000 DZD** one-time license = **~0.09% of annual revenue**, or **≈ 20–25% of a single student's yearly tuition.** That's within a director's discretionary spend — no board approval, no budget cycle. Even the full-system tier (~150k) is < 1 student's fees.

**Implication:** affordability is not the constraint — *proof* is. Hence the free pilot.

## Lens C — Comparable build / alternatives

- **Commission it custom from a developer:** a bespoke SIS ≈ 2–4 months of a mid-level dev at ~140,000–240,000 DZD/month → **~300,000–600,000 DZD** to build from scratch. *(Source: worldsalaries / maxishr, 2024–26.)*
- **Foreign SaaS school systems:** recurring per-student subscriptions, **forever**, not owned, rarely bilingual/dual-track, not in the Algerian bulletin format, sometimes billed in foreign currency.
- **Your offer:** a complete, owned, offline, bilingual, dual-track system for **~45,000–75,000 DZD once** — roughly **1/8th the cost of commissioning it**, with **no subscription**. This is your single strongest anchor: *"A developer would charge the school half a million dinars to build this; you own it for a fraction, once."*

## The pricing menu (structure)

1. **SIS core** — Students · Enrollment · Grades · Attendance · one-click bilingual bulletins. *List 75,000 DZD.*
2. **Full system** — adds Fees, Staff, Discipline, Inventory + **Curriculum Designer** + **auto Ministry reports**. *List 120,000–180,000 DZD.*
3. **Optional annual support** — updates, Ministry-format changes, phone support. *15–20% of licence/year.*
4. **Upgrade path** — SIS-core buyers upgrade to the full system later for the difference (natural upsell once the Curriculum Designer ships).

*One-time, paid on installation; offer 2 instalments if useful. You hand over the file — they own it.*

## Pilot-to-paid path

1. **Free pilot** — one trimester, 1–2 classes (or all grades for one trimester). **Zero cost.** Goal: produce real bulletins + attendance on their data.
2. **Decision gate** (end of trimester) — *"Do your staff want to keep it?"* A concrete yes/no on a real trimester, not a promise.
3. **Founding-customer license** — **45,000 DZD one-time, first-year support free**, in exchange for: a short written **testimonial**, permission to **name Allal as a reference**, and feedback for the roadmap.
4. **Later upsell** — Curriculum Designer + Ministry reporting as a paid add-on when those modules ship.
5. **Reference leverage** — Allal's bulletins + testimonial become the sales asset for the next schools (and the published Ta'allim paper adds academic credibility).

## What to actually say at the demo
- Don't open with a price. Open with the **free pilot**.
- If asked "how much?": *"One-time and affordable — you own it, no subscription. Let's let the pilot define the scope, then I'll price it fairly for Allal."*
- If pushed for a number: *"For a school like Allal, a one-time licence around **45,000 dinars**, and the pilot is free."*
- Always re-anchor on value: *"That's less than what you'd lose in staff hours in a single trimester — and a developer would charge ten times that to build it."*
